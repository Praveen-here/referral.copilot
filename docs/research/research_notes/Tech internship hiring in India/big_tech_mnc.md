# Big tech / MNC / GCC companies that hire technical interns and freshers in India (as of 23 Oct 2026)

Method note. "Verified" means I fetched the endpoint or page myself on 2026-10-23 and saw the data. "Reported" means a third-party source (usually a job aggregator) claims it and I could not confirm it at the source. I probed about 150 ATS boards with Python scripts (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters public JSON; Workday's public `wday/cxs` JSON search; Oracle HCM REST; Amazon `search.json`; Atlassian listings JSON; Google careers HTML). I filtered India by city names and entry roles by the words intern, graduate, new grad, university, campus, apprentice, trainee, "Engineer I", "SDE I". Keyword scans can miss postings with odd titles. A board showing 0 intern roles today means only that none were open on 2026-10-23. It does not mean the company never hires interns. Many programs open seasonally (see Q3).

Endpoint patterns used (all public, no login):
- Greenhouse: `https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{slug}/jobs`
- Lever: `https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/{slug}?mode=json`
- Ashby: `https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{slug}`
- SmartRecruiters: `https://api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/{slug}/postings?country=in`
- Workday (POST JSON `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":0,"searchText":"intern"}`): `https://{tenant}.{wdN}.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/{tenant}/{site}/jobs`
- Oracle HCM: `https://{host}/hcmRestApi/resources/latest/recruitingCEJobRequisitions?onlyData=true&expand=requisitionList&finder=findReqs;siteNumber={site},keyword="intern"`
- Amazon: `https://www.amazon.jobs/en/search.json?base_query=...&loc_query=India&country=IND`
- Atlassian: `https://www.atlassian.com/endpoint/careers/listings`

## Which big tech/MNCs actually hired technical interns and freshers in India in 2025-2026, and where do they post?

### Takeaway
I saw live India technical intern or new-grad postings on 2026-10-23 at Google, Amazon (SDE I), Stripe, Rubrik, HackerRank, HPE, Adobe, NVIDIA, Deutsche Bank, Bosch and Sumo Logic. Reports with credible detail, but no source confirmation, cover Microsoft, Walmart Global Tech, Goldman Sachs, Amazon's 6-month SDE intern and Google STEP. Many large GCCs (Databricks, Okta, MongoDB, Zscaler, ServiceNow, Pure Storage, JPMorgan, Oracle, Salesforce, Intel, PayPal, Mastercard, Target, Atlassian) have public JSON boards with dozens of India roles but no open intern listings today. Their intern hiring is seasonal or mostly on campus.

### Inferences
- The best automatable sources for India intern and new-grad roles are Workday `cxs` (HPE, Adobe, NVIDIA, Deutsche Bank, Citi, Micron, Salesforce, Intel and others), Greenhouse (Stripe, Rubrik, HackerRank, plus many GCCs with large India headcount) and amazon.jobs JSON. Google offers only HTML. Microsoft's API is locked behind auth or rate limits.
- Large GCCs with 50-100 open India roles but no interns today (Databricks, Okta, MongoDB, Zscaler, ServiceNow) are good referral targets for fresher and junior roles. Interns there mostly arrive through campus drives.
- PhD-heavy intern postings (Google, Adobe, NVIDIA) are the off-campus intern roles most often visible in October. Bachelor's-level intern roles in India appear more often via campus or seasonal windows.

### Gaps
- I could not verify Microsoft, Walmart, Uber, Apple, Qualcomm, AMD, Texas Instruments, Wells Fargo, Dell, Cisco, Intuit, Visa, PhonePe-style or Flipkart-style job feeds, because their endpoints failed or were not found.

- I had no Reddit thread access worth citing: searches returned aggregators, not r/developersIndia posts. I did not check Glassdoor or AmbitionBox intern reviews.

## Is hiring on-campus or off-campus, which programs exist, and when do they open?

### Takeaway
Most big-tech India intern hiring runs through campus drives, but several companies post off-campus openings anyone can apply to: Google, Amazon, Microsoft (reported), Stripe, HackerRank, HPE, Adobe, NVIDIA, Goldman, Walmart (reported). Postings cluster in Jun-Oct, for the next summer or the next Jan-Jun 6-month cycle.
