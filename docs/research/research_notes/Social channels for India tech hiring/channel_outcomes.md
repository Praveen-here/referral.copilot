# Job-search channel outcomes for Indian tech students (interns/freshers)

Labels: **[V]** I fetched the primary page and checked the figure myself. **[R]** the figure comes from a search snippet or a secondary or vendor source and I could not check it. All figures are global unless they are marked India. Research date: 2026-10-24. About 15 tool calls, so coverage is thin for some channels (see Gaps).

## Q1. Surveys and datasets on source-of-hire and conversion by channel

### Takeaway
The best conversion data comes from ATS vendors and is global, for all levels, and not specific to students. A referred application is about 13x more likely than a cold inbound application to reach an interview (40% vs 3%). Inbound conversion fell sharply from 2021 to 2024. I found no India-specific or student-specific dataset that breaks down hires by channel.

### Cited Findings
- **Ashby Talent Trends.** Data: Jan 2021 to Dec 2024, over 38M applications, 93K jobs, global, all levels. [V]
  - Share of applications: inbound 93.8%, sourced 4.8%, referral 1%, agency 0.4%.
  - Application to interview: internal 42%, referral 40%, agency 42%, sourced 25%, inbound 3%.
  - Interview to offer: internal 32%, referral 16%, agency 8%, sourced 6%, inbound 6%.
  - Over Q3 2021 to Q4 2024, inbound offer rate fell from about 0.7% to 0.2% (about 2 offers per 1,000 applications). Referral offer rate fell from 12.1% to 7.3%.
  - Referral share of applications fell from about 2% to under 1%.
  - No new-grad breakdown. — [Ashby referrals report](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals)
- **Ashby, other report.** Referrals are about 18% of hires across about 250K hires (Jan 2021 to Jun 2025). [R, search snippet] — [Ashby inbound](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/inbound)
- **Breezy 2024 Source of Hire.** Referrals were 12% of hires, based on 24M+ applications. [R] — [Breezy](https://breezy.hr/resources/hiring-reports/source-of-hire-report-2024)
- **Staffinghub 2024.** Referrals were 2% of applicants but 11% of hires. [R] — [Staffinghub](https://staffinghub.com/hiring/company-pages-referrals-result-in-more-hires-recruiting-metrics-report/)
- **ERIN 2024.** 1.1M referrals produced 146,689 hires, so about 13% of referrals were hired. [R] — [ERIN](https://erinapp.com/1-million-referrals)
- **NACE 2024 Student Survey** (US, Class of 2024). [R, snippet]
  - Of students who attended a career fair, over 45% got an interview offer afterwards and nearly 25% got a job offer.
  - About 29% attended no recruiting event at all. — [NACE](https://naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/student-attitudes/more-than-half-of-students-attended-a-career-fair-in-the-past-12-months)
- **NACE 2016** (US, older). Students who talked to current employees of an employer had a job-offer rate about 9 points higher. [R] — [NACE student attitudes](https://naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/student-attitudes/83bef2c3-619d-492b-bb34-36372f36a5e0)
- **NACE employer survey** (2017). Referrals from current or former interns are rated among the most effective ways to recruit interns. [R] — [NACE intern methods](https://ebiztest.naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/internships/the-most-used-most-effective-methods-for-recruiting-interns-co-ops/)
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply and job boards.** These numbers come from vendor blogs, not neutral research.
  - Huntr, about 600K tracked applications: LinkedIn applications reached an interview 3.10% of the time, against 11.29% for Google Jobs listings. [R] — [summary](https://bestjobsearchapps.com/articles/en/linkedin-vs-other-job-platforms-response-rates-volume-and-which-wins-for-your-job-search-in-2026); [Huntr](https://huntr.co/blog/why-linkedin-applications-go-nowhere)
  - Other vendors give Easy Apply anywhere from 1-2% to 3-13%, and company career pages anywhere from 3-5% to 15-30%. These estimates conflict. [R] — [loopcv](https://www.loopcv.pro/tr/guides/job-application-response-rate); [tryapt](https://www.tryapt.ai/blog/linkedin-easy-apply-vs-company-website)
- **India (old, not fresher-specific).** LinkedIn found at least 65% of recruiters in India use employee referral programmes, against about 32% in China and Australia. [R] — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/?p=12311)
- **India fresher hiring intent** (TeamLease EdTech).
  - H2 2025: 70%, from a survey of 1,065 employers. H1 2025: 74%, from 649 employers. H1 2024: 68%. [R]
  - The report has no channel breakdown. — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/recruiting-and-onboarding/e-commerce-and-tech-start-ups-push-fresher-hiring-intent-to-88percent-in-h2-2025-43039); [YourStory](https://ts.yourstory.com/2025/02/fresher-hiring-intent-jumps-first-half-2025-employers-teamlease)
- **India GCCs.** About 40% of entry-level hiring at Global Capability Centres (GCCs) is reportedly off-campus. This figure comes from a Times of India report via a prep blog, and I could not trace the original. [R, weak] — [placementpreparation.io](https://www.placementpreparation.io/blog/0ff-campus-recruitment-process-india/)

### Inferences
- The gap between channels, roughly 3% to interview for cold inbound against roughly 40% for referred or sourced candidates, is the strongest number available. It probably holds for Indian freshers too, since employers there use referrals heavily, but no data confirms it for that group.
- Easy Apply and careers-page rates come from vendors that sell auto-apply tools. Treat them as rough direction: mass-apply channels convert in the low single digits.

### Gaps
- I found no Internshala, Unstop, Naukri or Handshake source-of-hire breakdown by channel for students. I also found no Jobvite or Greenhouse new-grad split.
- I did not find the Internshala report PDF, and I found no figure for applications received per internship.

## Q2. Cold email to Indian startup founders: reply rates and write-ups

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous data on this. The only numbers are self-reported anecdotes, mostly from outside India: about 11% positive replies (150 emails sent) and one outlier at 72% (485 emails). One India vendor blog claims 12-18% response for cold email against 2-4% for portals, with no stated method.

### Cited Findings
- A student on Reddit reported sending 150 cold emails over 6 weeks and getting 17 positive responses (11.3%). Another student, in a Venture Twins case study, sent 485 cold emails and got 350 responses (72%). [R, search-summary snippets; I did not see the original posts] — [firstsales.io](https://firstsales.io/blog/cold-emails-for-internships/) and related results
- Instantly.ai reports an average cold email reply rate of 3.43%. This is a sales-email benchmark, not specific to jobs. [R] — [firstsales.io](https://firstsales.io/blog/cold-emails-for-internships/)
- JobRise, an India vendor blog, claims cold email to hiring managers gets a 12-18% response rate against 2-4% for portal applications. It states no method. [R, weak] — [JobRise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/how-to-write-cold-email-recruiter/)
- Avi Vashishta's blog lists 106 AI startups to cold email for internships (India, remote, and live openings). It sources them from the YC directory, PrepNPlaced and Wellfound India listings, and includes a sample email. [R] — [avivashishta.com](https://www.avivashishta.com/blog/106-ai-startups-to-cold-email-for-internship)
- Common advice for structuring the email: [R]
  - Keep it to 50-125 words.
  - Offer a small, concrete project rather than asking for "any role".
  - Follow up, since most replies come after the first email.
  - Sources: [whali](https://whali.com/blog/cold-email-internship-step-by-step); [Mailmeteor](https://mailmeteor.com/cold-email/internship); [eChai](https://echai.ventures/startingup/cold-outreach/how-do-i-write-a-cold-email-that-people-actually-reply-to)
- Rohan Gupta (US, new grad) traces both of his early-stage startup roles to cold emails. [R] — [Substack](https://rohangupta2036.substack.com/p/how-to-land-the-right-early-stage)
- Skeptics on Blind argue that cold outreach rarely works where a formal intern program exists. [R, opinion] — [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/cs-undergrad-looking-for-adviceinternships-apstn2iw)

### Inferences
- Cold email works best at small startups with no intern program. At companies with a structured program, it is weaker than a referral or a hiring challenge.
- Even the anecdotal figures (about 10% positive replies) beat inbound applications (3% to interview, per Ashby). They are not comparable metrics, though.

### Gaps
- My searches did not surface a r/developersIndia or r/Indian_Academia thread with funnel numbers (sent, replied, interviewed, offered). Reddit can only be reached through snippets.

## Q3. Wellfound and YC Work at a Startup for Indian students

### Takeaway
There is little evidence either way. YC's Work at a Startup has listed some India internships (2019). Anecdotes say Wellfound responses are slow and patchy, and few listed companies hire interns. India-based YC startups have also become rarer since 2023.

### Cited Findings
- **YC 2019 intern program.** About 50 companies took part. Most roles were in the Bay Area and NYC, with some internships in India. One profile was sent to all participating startups. [R, snippet of YC blog] — [YC blog](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/intern-at-a-yc-company-2019)
- **Indian/SEA startups in YC batches** (TechCrunch, Sept 2023): [R]
  - W21: 44
  - S21: 33
  - W22: 37
  - S22: 20
  - The batch before S23: 10
  - S23: 1
  - Note: some Indian startups incorporate in the US and may not be counted. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/19/yc-says-visa-challenges-hampering-participation-of-international-founders)
- **Wellfound responsiveness** (Blind, US undergrads). [R, anecdote] — [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/any-start-ups-that-are-still-hiring-swes-in-the-us-o3exbwds)
  - "hardly get any response"
  - Some companies don't respond for months
  - "hardly any company on that list is willing/able to hire interns"

### Inferences
- For Indian students, Wellfound and YC Work at a Startup work best as lead lists (who is hiring, who the founder is). The student would then email the founder directly, rather than relying on the platform's apply button.
- Fewer India-based YC startups means fewer local, in-office YC internships. Remote roles for US YC startups exist but are uncommon for interns.

### Gaps
- I found no Wellfound or YC data on international or India applicants: response rates, number of India internship listings, or remote-intern policy as of 2026.

## Q4. Hackathons, hiring challenges and open source (Unstop, GRiD, Juspay, GSoC)

### Takeaway
Hiring challenges draw huge numbers of entrants (hundreds of thousands) but produce hires in the hundreds. Per entrant the conversion is very low, under about 0.1-1%, though reaching the finals is a strong signal. GSoC has no published hiring-outcome data.

### Cited Findings
- **Unstop case-study headline:** "Walmart Hires 300+, Flipkart 166, Intellect 302 & Juspay Pushes PPIs To 2610+ Through Hiring Challenges". A PPI is a pre-placement interview. [V, the headline is on the page; I could not read the article body]
  - The URL slug says "interview offers to 2473 engineers", which conflicts with 2610+.
  - These are vendor marketing numbers. — [Unstop case studies](https://unstop.com/category/unstop-case-studies)
- **Flipkart GRiD 6.0:** 5.17 lakh+ (517K+) registrations. [V, title only] — [Unstop case studies](https://unstop.com/category/unstop-case-studies)
  - Implied conversion: if the 166 Flipkart hires came from GRiD-scale pools, that is about 0.03% of registrants. This is my inference; the 166 may span several challenges.
- **GRiD 4.0:** three rounds. All finalists were offered internship interviews. [R] — [Unstop blog](https://unstop.com/blog/win-cash-prizes-and-tech-internships-through-flipkart-hackathon/amp)
- **Juspay Hiring Challenge 2025** (2026 graduating B.Tech): [R]
  - 202,147 registrations.
  - Stages: MCQ, then coding, then interview.
  - Offer: internship at about Rs 40K a month, which can convert to a full-time SDE role at about Rs 21-27 LPA. These pay figures come from aggregator sites.
  - The 2022 edition had 90K+ registrations. — [Unstop listing](https://unstop.com/competitions/juspay-hiring-challenge-2025-juspay-1466934/amp); [govtinternship](https://govtinternship.com/juspay-internship/); [PrepInsta](https://prepinsta.com/juspay-hiring-challenge/)
- **Internshala Trends Report 2024 (India):** [R]
  - Internship listings up 25% year on year.
  - 22-23% of internships offer a pre-placement offer (PPO).
  - Average stipend Rs 8,000 a month.
  - Engineering is 20% of listings.
  - Fresh graduates are 30% of shortlisted candidates. — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/internshala-internship-trends-report-2024-25-percent-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024/amp); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/internship-opportunities-surge-by-25-in-2024-report-50595.htm)
- **GSoC:** [R]
  - India had the most accepted students in 2014: 401, against 167 for the US.
  - IIIT Hyderabad and BITS Pilani were the top schools in 2017.
  - No post-program hiring statistics exist. The career value claims are anecdotal. — [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com/blog/401-indian-gsocers-got-make-count); [Google OSS blog 2018](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2018/06/google-summer-of-code-2018-statistics-part-2.html)

### Inferences
- Hiring challenges are a lottery at the registration stage but a referral-like path at the finalist stage, where interviews are guaranteed. For students without a referral, they are the main fair door into large Indian tech companies (Flipkart, Walmart, Juspay).
- About 22% of Internshala internships come with a PPO offer. So a paid internship, even from a mass portal, is a real route to a full-time offer at small companies. This assumes the PPO is actually honored, which is unverified.

### Gaps
- No PPO conversion rate from hiring challenges, no GRiD hire counts by season, and no Unstop methodology.
- No outcome data for GSoC or other open-source programs (LFX, MLH Fellowship).

## Q5. Referrals for interns in India, and how small startups find interns

### Takeaway
Referrals are the highest-converting non-internal channel in global data, and Indian recruiters use referral programmes heavily. No India intern-specific number exists. Small startups mostly hire interns through their network, the founders' social media, short assignments, and Internshala. The evidence for that last point comes from founder accounts and is qualitative.

### Cited Findings
- **Global referral conversion:** 40% of referred applications reach an interview, and 16% of referred interviews end in an offer, against 3% and 6% for inbound (Ashby, 38M applications). [V] — [Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/referrals)
- **India:** at least 65% of recruiters use referral programmes (LinkedIn survey, old, all levels). [R] — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/?p=12311)
- **Internshala founder Sarvesh Agrawal** largely built the company with interns hired through his own platform. He recommends a short assignment to test how sincere an applicant is, and values applicants who send feedback on the product. [R] — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2022/07/internshala-blog-profitable-intership-skilling-career-tech-startup); [LinkedIn Pulse snippet only, not fetched](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/how-hire-best-interns-your-start-up-sarvesh-agrawal)
- **Nikhil Jois (Eventosaur co-founder)** gets many internship requests because he is active on social media, and says warm referrals make a positive response more likely. Not confirmed as an Indian founder. [R] — [Medium](https://medium.com/@nikhiljoisr/the-definitive-guide-to-landing-an-internship-at-a-startup-8fe3fb407173)
- **Founders Network guide:** recommends startups hire interns through their network and referrals, university partnerships, and online platforms. [R] — [Founders Network](https://foundersnetwork.com/ultimate-guide-to-hiring-interns-for-startups/)
- **Cutshort and Instahyre:** only vendor claims, no independent data. [R]
  - Instahyre: personalized emails get about 5x the response of generic emails.
  - Cutshort (2019): employers accept about 70% of the matches it shows them.
  - Sources: [Instahyre help](https://help.instahyre.com/en/article/email-tips-best-practices-r49let/); [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/naukri-alternatives)

### Inferences
- **Suggested channel ranking for an Indian intern or fresher.** This is my synthesis, not measured data.
  1. Referral, or a warm intro from an alumnus or employee.
  2. Direct email to the founder or engineer at a small startup, with a concrete project or proof of work.
  3. Finals of a hiring challenge (GRiD, Juspay, Unstop).
  4. Campus or off-campus drives.
  5. Curated platforms (Instahyre, Cutshort, Wellfound), where employers make the first move.
  6. Internshala, for volume at small companies.
  7. Easy Apply or a careers page alone (about 3% to interview).
- For the product, this supports referrals first and alumni first. It also suggests adding a founder or engineer direct-email path for startups under about 50 people, where no formal referral program exists.

### Gaps
- No data on intern hires coming from social-media hiring posts (X/Twitter, LinkedIn posts) in India.
- No India figure for the share of intern hires that come through referrals.
- No figures for campus or off-campus drives beyond the weak GCC claim of about 40% off-campus.
