# Tech internship and fresher hiring in India: what students report works (community and strategy)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Method: web search plus page fetches (about 17 tool calls). Note on evidence: Reddit (r/developersIndia and others) could not be fetched directly (old.reddit.com blocked for the fetch tool), and `site:reddit.com` searches returned no Reddit pages. X/Twitter threads are poorly indexed. So first-hand Indian student anecdotes in these notes are thin. Most "data" below comes from company-run surveys reported in the press (Internshala, TeamLease EdTech, Unstop, Apna, LocalCircles). Those surveys are not peer-reviewed and come from firms that sell hiring or ed-tech services. Treat them as directional.

## Q1. Do Indian startups hire students (still in college) for long, remote or part-time internships and full-time roles? Do they care less about degree or experience?

### Takeaway
Yes. Startups and e-commerce or tech firms report the highest fresher hiring intent in India (88% in H2 2025, TeamLease, 1,065 employers). Startup listings routinely offer 3 to 6 month internships, many remote, open to current students and with PPO (pre-placement offer, i.e. a job offer to the intern) paths. "Proof of work" is often asked for, but usually on top of degree or batch requirements, not in place of them.

### Cited Findings
**Data (surveys and platform reports)**
- TeamLease EdTech Career Outlook Report HY2 2025 (Jul to Dec 2025). Fresher hiring intent was highest in e-commerce and tech startups at **88%**, then retail 87% and manufacturing 82%. Overall intent fell to **70%** from 74% in H1 2025, which the report attributes to AI-driven restructuring, trade uncertainty and retaining experienced staff. Survey ran May to July 2025, **n = 1,065 employers**. — [PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/recruiting-and-onboarding/e-commerce-and-tech-start-ups-push-fresher-hiring-intent-to-88percent-in-h2-2025-43039); [The Week / PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/08/19/dcm70-biz-freshers-hiring-report.html)
- Same report, by city: Bengaluru **81%**, Mumbai 67%, Chennai 59%. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/e-commerce-tech-startups-lead-fresher-hiring-with-88-intent-in-h2-2025-teamlease-report-79134.htm)
- Internshala Internship Trends Report 2024, published Dec 2024 (platform data, not a survey):
  - Internship openings grew **25%** over 2023 and **135% since 2020**.
  - Engineering made up **20%** of openings and management 42%.
  - Average stipend was **Rs 8,000/month**, with top offers up to Rs 1 lakh.
  - About **49%** of internships were work-from-home (48% in 2023).
  - PPO share was reported as **22%** by [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/internshala-internship-trends-report-2024-25-percent-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024) and **23%** by other outlets. The coverage conflicts, so this is unresolved.
  - Sources: [BW People](https://www.bwpeople.in/article/25-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024-internshala-report-542289); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/internship-opportunities-surge-by-25-in-2024-report-50595.htm)
- Conflicting stipend figure: a March 2025 report puts India's average internship stipend at **Rs 25,432/month**. This is far above Internshala's Rs 8,000, and the methodology isn't given. — [The Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2025/3/28/-Internship-opportunities-in-India-double-in-3-years-.html)
- Live Internshala listings (snapshot Oct 2026): engineering internships pay "typically ₹1,000 to ₹1,25,000 per month". Examples include remote web development at ₹15k to 25k/month and remote full stack (React/Tailwind for a SaaS) at ₹8k to 14k/month. Some listings pay as little as ₹1,000. — [Internshala engineering](https://internshala.com/internships/engineering-internship/); [Internshala WFH engineering](https://internshala.com/internships/work-from-home-engineering-internships/)
- Unstop Talent Report 2025 (survey of **30,000+ Gen Z** and **700 HR leaders**):
  - **83%** of engineering graduates had no job or internship offer.
  - **60%** of students from premier engineering schools had no job offer in hand.
  - Sources: [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/jobs/story/83-engineering-46-b-school-grads-remain-without-a-job-internship-offer-unstop-report-468828-2025-03-21); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/60-of-students-from-premier-engineering-schools-do-not-have-a-job-offer-in-hand-unstop-talent-report-2025/)
  - Note: this is a vendor survey, and "graduates" probably means students at the time of the survey.
- Unstop Talent Report 2026 (**37,000+ students, 500 HR leaders**): 88% of companies were "in hiring mode" and 90% were keeping or raising hiring budgets. — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/trending/only-36-hr-leaders-ready-for-gen-z-hiring-majority-of-students-remain-unplaced-report-92558.htm)

**Listings and curated job newsletters (evidence of practice, not statistics)**
- The First Pass #27 (21 Jun 2026) is a curated list of roughly 20 Indian fresher and intern roles.
  - **Duration:** most internships run 3 to 6 months (SuperBryn AI and Potpie 6 months, Nua 3 to 6 months).
  - **Remote:** remote roles appear, e.g. a remote software intern at Lane, and a stealth founder's office role that is "Remote-first with only one in-person meeting a week".
  - **Eligibility:** "Freshers and final-year students"; Lane targets the "2026–2028 batches", which means current students. A quant role prefers CGPA 8.5+.
  - **Stipends:** Lane software intern ₹15k, PlanYs ₹30k, Scaler AI engineer intern ₹50k to 60k with PPO.
  - Source: [The First Pass #27](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-27-pick-a-startup)
- Startup 6-month listings with explicit conversion paths:
  - A Revformance full-stack intern role is "6-month … path to a full-time offer".
  - A Terralogic listing says "opportunity for full-time employment based on … performance evaluation".
  - Some 3 or 6 month listings are unpaid ("no stipend will be provided").
  - Sources: [Glassdoor India 6-month internships](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/internship-6-month-jobs-SRCH_KO0,18.htm); [Indeed India](https://in.indeed.com/q-6-months-internship-for-final-year-students-jobs.html)
- A Sprinto AI Implementation Intern role (6 months) is open to "final-semester or completed" candidates. — [PrepnPlaced](https://www.prepnplaced.com/apprenticeships/internships) (aggregator, unverified)
- A Bengaluru AI founder's-office intern listing asks for a degree or final-semester standing **and** for proof of work (GitHub repos, live demos, deployed tools). — [jobfound.org listing](https://jobfound.org/job/waterscience-is-hiring-for-founders-office-intern-ai-tech-bengaluru-karnataka-india-india-21-october-2026)

**Opinion**
- An Indian angel investor's column (Dr. Malpani) advises bootstrapped founders to screen on "Public portfolios: Ask them to show past work—GitHub projects, blog posts, videos, designs". It also says applications should not be limited to particular colleges. — [Indian Angel blog](https://www.indianangel.in/proof-of-work-not-degrees-how-bootstrapped-founders-should-hire-talent/)

### Inferences
- For a student, a 6-month (often Jan to Jun "winter/semester-off") or remote internship at a startup is a normal, widely listed format. A PPO conversion path is common enough to ask about.
- Startups appear to weigh skills and proof of work more than big firms do. But listings still filter by batch, degree and sometimes CGPA, so "startups don't care about degree" is overstated.
- Stipend spread is very wide (₹1k to ₹1 lakh+), so pay is a useful legitimacy and quality filter.

### Gaps
- No 2025 Internshala annual trends report was found; the latest is the 2024 one.
- No NASSCOM, Naukri or LinkedIn India report specific to startup intern hiring was found.
- No data on part-time internships held during the semester.
- No founder survey on degree vs proof of work in India.

## Q2. Does cold-messaging founders or CTOs at small Indian startups work? Reply rates, message shapes, proof of work vs resume

### Takeaway
There is no India-specific reply-rate data. The only number found is a non-India Reddit anecdote of an 11% positive-reply rate. Indian guides and listings agree on the approach: at small startups, email the founder or CTO directly, keep it short, and lead with GitHub, a project or a demo. Several Indian startup listings explicitly ask applicants to email a GitHub profile or a project rather than only a resume.

### Cited Findings
**Anecdotes (weak, mostly non-India)**
- A student on r/internships (not India-specific, cited second-hand by a vendor blog) reportedly sent **150 cold emails over 6 weeks** and got **17 positive responses (11.3%)**. The surrounding benchmarks in that blog are vendor marketing. — [firstsales.io](https://firstsales.io/blog/cold-emails-for-internships/)
- On Blind, one poster reported **14 replies from 49 cold applications** (US context). Another doubted cold outreach for internships "unless you want to be taken advantage of", noting that most organisations run formal intern programs. — [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/cold-applications-vkjuod2p); [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/reach-out-vs-cold-applications-n2mcw5oy)
- A Hacker News commenter said that if you're recruiting for a "generic unknown startup expect your response rates to be lower". This is about outreach from recruiters, not to them. — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7917001)

**India-specific guidance and practice**
- An Indian (Hinglish) internship guide says to target "Startups: Founder ya CTO ko. Startups mein hiring process informal hota hai. Founder directly decide karta hai." It recommends HR or an engineering manager at mid-size firms. — [Jobrise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/internship-kaise-milegi-tips/)
- What Indian startup listings ask for:
  - An S45 SWE internship (Bengaluru) asks applicants to email a GitHub profile or a project. — [The First Pass #28](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-28-the-vibe-shift)
  - PlanYs asks for "LeetCode/Codeforces/CodeChef/HackerRank profile, GitHub, and notable projects".
  - Passionfruit asks for "resume + a short note on why you'd be a great fit".
  - Source for PlanYs and Passionfruit: [The First Pass #27](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-27-pick-a-startup)
- A cold-email guide (US, dated) says the subject line matters most because founders are "buried in email clutter". It recommends a clear ask plus a concrete offer of value. — [WeWork Ideas](https://www.wework.com/ideas/?p=5984)

### Inferences
- The best-supported message shape for an Indian startup founder:
  - one or two lines on why this company;
  - a link to a relevant project, demo or GitHub repo;
  - a specific ask (an internship of N months, remote or on-site, with a start date);
  - a resume attached or linked.
- Being able to say "available 6 months, full-time, from Jan" is probably a strong hook, since startups list mostly 3 to 6 month roles. This is an inference from the listings, not tested data.

### Gaps
- No Indian reply-rate data for cold email or DMs to founders.
- No Reddit or X first-hand Indian threads could be retrieved. Reddit is blocked for the fetch tool, and X is poorly indexed.

## Q3. WhatsApp vs LinkedIn vs email vs X for reaching alumni and startup people in India; communities as job sources

### Takeaway
No reply-rate data by channel was found for India. Advice-level consensus says WhatsApp and Telegram job groups are fast but full of junk and scams. A May 2025 LocalCircles survey found 42% of Indian WhatsApp users had received fake job offers demanding payment. Hackathons and competitions are students' top-ranked engagement channel (Unstop 2026), but only 25% of recruiters treat them as a primary hiring tool (Unstop 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **LocalCircles (May 2025):** 42% of WhatsApp users in India received fake job offers demanding payments. Reported second-hand; sample size not given in the coverage. — [mediaindia.eu summary / search coverage](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)
- A college-oriented Indian guide describes WhatsApp and Telegram groups as "genuinely fast on openings, saturated with junk". This is opinion. — [annauniversityplus.com](https://annauniversityplus.com/blog/job-portals-and-platforms-for-freshers)
- **IIT Bhubaneswar** warned that a fraudulent internship scheme circulating through **WhatsApp groups** was misusing the name of its Summer Internship Programme 2025. — [IIT Bhubaneswar notice](https://www.iitbbs.ac.in/?p=31569)
- Indian cyber experts say scammers also use **LinkedIn**, with fake profiles and startup pages that harvest resumes. — [Dinamalar](https://www.dinamalar.com/news/kalvimalar-news-en/fraudsters-target-college-students-with-fake-internship-offers-warn-cyber-experts/57787)
- **Unstop Talent Report 2025:** 70% of Gen Z engage in case studies, ideathons and simulations, but only **25%** of recruiters consider these a primary hiring tool. — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/jobs/story/83-engineering-46-b-school-grads-remain-without-a-job-internship-offer-unstop-report-468828-2025-03-21)
- **Unstop Talent Report 2026:** students rank competitions and hackathons as the top engagement channel across streams. — [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/trending/only-36-hr-leaders-ready-for-gen-z-hiring-majority-of-students-remain-unplaced-report-92558.htm)

### Inferences
- Alumni WhatsApp groups (your own college, verified members) are probably higher-signal than open "job alert" groups. That is because the scam warnings centre on unknown groups and impersonation. This is not measured.
- Email to a founder's work address, or a LinkedIn connection request with a note, are the channels Indian listings and guides refer to most. X and Discord are absent from the sources found.

### Gaps
- No reply-rate comparison across WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email and X for India.
- No data on Discord or Slack developer communities (e.g. hackathon communities) as job sources in India.

## Q4. Which off-campus channels actually led to tech internships (referrals, employee LinkedIn posts, Wellfound, Internshala, Unstop, hiring contests, GSoC/LFX, cold email)?

### Takeaway
No survey ranking channels by actual offers for Indian tech students was found. Evidence by channel:

| Channel | Evidence found |
|---|---|
| Internshala | Strong volume data |
| Unstop | Strong participation data, weak conversion to hiring |
| Wellfound | Described as the startup channel, only third-party descriptions |
| Curated newsletters | Present (e.g. The First Pass) |
| GSoC | Selection stories, not job-outcome data |
| Referrals | Widely claimed to boost callbacks, but no Indian student data |

### Cited Findings
- **Internshala** is a high-volume channel: openings grew 25% in 2024, 20% of them in engineering, and 49% were WFH. — [BW People](https://www.bwpeople.in/article/25-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024-internshala-report-542289)
- **Unstop** hackathons and competitions: 70% of Gen Z participate, but only 25% of recruiters use them as a primary hiring tool (2025, n = 30k+ / 700 HR). — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/jobs/story/83-engineering-46-b-school-grads-remain-without-a-job-internship-offer-unstop-report-468828-2025-03-21)
- **Wellfound** is described as a startup-hiring platform used by "over 100,000 startups worldwide". It is suited to startup or remote internships of 2 to 6 months with no strict year-of-study filter, and its stipend range (unofficial) is ₹5k to 50k. All of this comes from a third-party blog, so it is unverified. — [theinternship.in](https://theinternship.in/wellfound-intern/)
- **GSoC:**
  - In 2024, a first-year Newton School of Technology student was selected (NumFOCUS/JupyterLab), one of **1,220 contributors from 43,984 applicants**. GSoC is paid, and the article frames it as a "1.25 lakh internship". This shows selection, not a later job offer. — [The Week / PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2024/05/11/dcm9-newton-school-of-technology.html)
  - An older HackerEarth piece quotes an Indian GSoCer: "5,500 USD is a lot of money", and most students apply for that money; many stop contributing after 3 months. This is dated. — [HackerEarth](https://hackerearth.com/blog/developers/401-indian-gsocers-got-make-count/amp)
- **Off-campus share:** a blog citing a Times of India report says almost **40%** of entry-level hiring at India's GCCs (global capability centres) now happens off-campus. This is second-hand and the original was not verified. — [placementpreparation.io](https://www.placementpreparation.io/blog/off-campus-recruitment-process-india/)
- **Curated fresher-role newsletters** (e.g. The First Pass) list founder's-office and SWE intern roles with direct application links or founder email. — [The First Pass #27](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-27-pick-a-startup)

### Inferences
- For startup roles, the realistic channel mix is Wellfound, Internshala (filtered by stipend), curated newsletters and direct founder email. For big product firms, it is the formal intern programs plus employee referrals.
- GSoC/LFX is best seen as proof of work that helps with every other channel, not as a direct hiring pipeline. No outcome data supports a direct pipeline.

### Gaps
- No survey of "which channel produced your offer" for Indian tech students.
- No LFX outcome data.
- No data on hiring contests' conversion rates, e.g. company coding contests.

## Q5. Referral reality in India: do referrals for interns or freshers get interviews? Referral bonuses? Ghosting, fake postings, unpaid or fee-charging internships, and how to spot genuine postings

### Takeaway
Indian employers use referrals heavily: about 65% of recruiters (LinkedIn data cited in 2015), and some firms fill 70 to 90% of hires this way. But no data was found on referral-to-interview rates for interns or freshers, or on referral bonuses for intern hires. Scam and ghosting evidence is strong:
- 42% of WhatsApp users got fake paid job offers (LocalCircles 2025).
- Kroll estimates about 20% of Indian job postings are suspected fake.
- 73% of respondents in a 2024 online poll had been ghosted.
- Fee-charging "internships" are explicitly warned against by IIT Bhubaneswar, St. Xavier's Jaipur and AICTE.

### Cited Findings
**Referrals**
- About **65%** of Indian organisations use employee referrals, against a 39% global average. This is a LinkedIn recruiting trends survey cited in **2015**, so it is old. — [PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/amp-recruitment-least-65-recruiters-india-use-employee-referral-programmes-12311)
- **Howden India** gets 70% of hires through referrals (Oct 2025, an insurance firm, single company). — [HR Katha](https://www.hrkatha.com/features/how-howden-india-gets-70-of-hires-through-employee-referrals/)
- **G+D MS India** attracts about 90% of talent through referrals (2022, single company). — [HR Katha](https://www.hrkatha.com/features/this-company-attracts-90-of-talent-through-referrals/)
- Guides claim referrals "materially boost callback rates" but cite no data. Some blogs give a figure of about 21% of hires from referrals with no primary source. Weak evidence. — [weekday.works](https://www.weekday.works/post/recruitment-channels-in-india)

**Ghosting and fake postings**
- A Business Standard online survey (**n = 1,000**, April 2024, non-representative) found **73%** of respondents had been ghosted by potential employers. — [mediaindia.eu](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)
- Kroll estimates **~20%** of Indian job postings are suspected fake. — [mediaindia.eu / search coverage](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)
- A JobTether vendor analysis of **29,815** live Indian postings (Sep 2026) found about **1 in 4 was older than 90 days**. This is a single vendor's measurement. — [JobTether](https://jobtether.com/blog/ghost-jobs-india)
- The I4C (Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre) and CERT-In report that digital frauds, including fake job offers, cost Indians **₹4,245 crore** in the first 10 months of FY2024-25. — [dailyfinancial.in](https://dailyfinancial.in/caught-in-a-trap-fake-job-scams-india-drain-%E2%82%B94245-crore-stay-safe/)

**Fee-charging internship scams and how to spot them**
- **Chennai case:** cybersecurity students were offered a ₹15,000 stipend. After a video interview they were asked to pay ₹2,000 via UPI for a "police clearance certificate", and the offer was confirmed as fraud. — [Dinamalar](https://www.dinamalar.com/news/kalvimalar-news-en/fraudsters-target-college-students-with-fake-internship-offers-warn-cyber-experts/57787)
- **Common tactics:**
  - students are "hired" with no interview;
  - they are asked for a "security deposit" or about ₹10,000 for "training materials";
  - they are asked for a registration fee to a personal UPI ID or QR code.
  - Source: [CyberPeace](https://cyberpeace.org/resources/blogs/internship-scams-exposing-financial-frauds-targeting-students). One CyberPeace page has placeholder references, so treat its details cautiously.
- **St. Xavier's College Jaipur** advisory (Dec 2025) says outside internship emails demanding registration or participation fees are not endorsed. — [SXC Jaipur PDF](https://sxcjpr.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Notice-regarding-Unauthorized-Internship-Offers-and-Fraudulent-Emails-2025.pdf)
- **AICTE:** official internships go through the AICTE National Internship Portal, which is free for students. — [CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/features/aicte-internship-scams-new-2026-rules-to-identify-fake-offers-and-protect-your-career-011-65999.html)
- **Reporting:** National Cyber Crime Helpline **1930**.
- **Unpaid:** some startup 3 or 6 month listings state no stipend. — [Glassdoor India](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/internship-6-month-jobs-SRCH_KO0,18.htm)

### Inferences
Red flags supported by the sources:
- any fee or deposit;
- payment to a personal UPI ID;
- an offer with no interview;
- contact only via WhatsApp or Telegram, or a recruiter with a new LinkedIn profile or company page;
- impersonation of an IIT, AICTE or government body;
- a posting more than 90 days old (ghost-job risk).

### Gaps
- No data on referral-to-interview conversion for Indian interns or freshers.
- No data on whether Indian firms pay referral bonuses for intern hires.

## Q6. Timing: when do Indian companies open summer and winter internships and fresher off-campus hiring?

### Takeaway
By one 2026 calendar (typical patterns, not confirmed dates):
- **Big-tech and bank summer internships** (for the next year) open **August to October**, and FAANG-style intern applications run August to December.
- **Global banks** open nearly a year ahead; Goldman Sachs India opened its 2027 Summer Analyst applications on 1 July 2026.
- **Mass fresher off-campus drives** run January to May (service firms, TCS NQT in Feb to Mar).
- **Product and startup fresher hiring** peaks in July and again from September.
- **December** is the slowest month.
- **Startups and Internshala listings** hire year-round.

### Cited Findings
Let's Code "India Off-Campus Hiring Calendar 2026" (27 Apr 2026, "typical patterns"). Source for all items in this list: [Let's Code](https://www.lets-code.co.in/blogs/india-off-campus-hiring-calendar-2026/)
- **Jan to Mar:**
  - service and Big 4 fresher drives (TCS, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture);
  - TCS NQT registration opens 18 Feb, in-centre tests start 10 Mar, registration closes 20 Mar.
- **Apr to May:** product and MNC fresher roles (Amazon SDE-I, Salesforce, Oracle, SAP Labs, PayPal, Adobe, Intuit, Cisco).
- **Jun:** PPO conversions and semiconductor hiring (NVIDIA, Qualcomm, TI, Intel Graduate Intern).
- **Jul:**
  - campus season starts;
  - fresher drives at Microsoft, Google, Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, Meesho;
  - "FAANG+ intern applications typically open August–December".
- **Aug:** summer 2027 internships are live from August to October. Named programs: Microsoft Engage, Google STEP, Amazon SDE Intern, Goldman Sachs, JPMC Code for Good, Morgan Stanley.
- **Sep:** second peak, including PhonePe, Razorpay, Zoho, Freshworks, CRED, Postman, Groww and Zerodha.
- **Oct:** quant and finance (D.E. Shaw, Arcesium, Optiver, Jane Street, Graviton).
- **Nov:** last push (Atlassian, Uber, Walmart Global Tech, Salesforce winter intake).
- **Dec:** slowest; some startups and mid-tier firms post quietly.

Other sources:
- A guide says structured programs (banks, consulting, big product firms) open summer 2026 applications in Nov to Dec of the prior year, with **Jan to Feb as peak season**. This conflicts in part with the August-to-October timing above; big tech likely opens earlier than mid-size firms. — [Saarthi](https://joinsaarthi.com/blogs/summer-internship-2026-india-complete-application-guide)
- Global banks hire summer interns nearly a year ahead; Goldman Sachs opened India 2027 Summer Analyst applications on 1 Jul 2026, with rolling review. — [QuintEdge](https://quintedge.com/blog/investment-banking-internships-india)
- Examples of deadlines: Tata Global Internships summer 2026 closed 12 Jan 2026, and IIT Madras WSAI summer 2026 closed 16 Feb 2026. — [Tufts careers](https://careers.tufts.edu/blog/2025/12/08/tatas-global-internships/); [IIT Madras WSAI](https://wsai.iitm.ac.in/internships/)
- Six-month winter internships (Jan to Jun) are a recognised format, with lists of companies that offer them. — [Medium list](https://avlmp2003.medium.com/the-ultimate-list-companies-hiring-for-6-month-winter-internships-jan-june-847975b9363e) (not fetched; unverified)

### Inferences
- As of Oct 2026, summer 2027 big-tech intern windows are already open or closing.
- Startups and 6-month Jan-start internships are the live opportunity for Nov to Dec outreach.
- Off-campus fresher drives for the 2027 batch should be expected from Jan 2027.

### Gaps
- The calendar is a blog's "typical pattern", not company-confirmed dates.
- No sourced data on when Indian startups specifically post winter or 6-month internships. The Medium list was not verified.
