# Recently funded Indian tech startups (Jun to Oct 2026): hiring signals and automatable funding feeds

Research date: 2026-10-23. How it was done: I read the weekly funding roundups (Inc42, Entrackr) and pulled company names, amounts and dates from them. I then queried the four public ATS JSON endpoints (Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse, Workable) with curl, using slugs guessed from each company name, and I loaded careers pages to see which ATS they link to. "Verified" means I fetched the endpoint or page myself on 2026-10-23. "Reported" means the claim comes from a news article.

## Which Indian tech startups raised funding between ~April and October 2026, and does each have a public careers page / ATS with India technical intern or entry-level roles?

### Takeaway
The weekly roundups give 100+ named deals for Jun to Oct 2026. Most are D2C, EV or other non-software companies. This list is tech-leaning: 40 companies from five roundups. Only a few have a public JSON job board I could verify. Sarvam and Ema (Ashby) list India engineering roles. RevRag.AI's own careers page lists 6-month technical internships in Bengaluru with a PPO. Clueso's Ashby board has no technical roles. Several others use Keka or Zoho Recruit careers pages, which have no simple public JSON feed. For most companies, every guessed ATS slug returned 404.

**Slug collisions (an important lesson for automation):** Ashby returned HTTP 200 for `nudge`, `confido`, `circle` and `sol`, but these boards belong to different companies:
- `nudge`: a San Francisco neurotech company. Its jobs include MR Physicist and Histotechnologist.
- `confido`: a New York CPG/AI company. All its jobs are listed as "NYC Office".
- `circle`: a global company hiring Brand Designer and Translator roles.
- `sol`: San Mateo, with a Field HR Product Architect role.

None of these is the Indian startup with the same name. Workable returned `{"name":"Crest"}`, `{"name":"DSW"}`, `{"name":"Centricity"}`, `{"name":"origin"}` and `{"name":"AutoVRse"}` with empty job lists. Only AutoVRse matches the startup's name exactly, and even that one is unconfirmed. [Ashby nudge](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/nudge), [Ashby confido](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/confido)

### Inferences
- Probing guessed ATS slugs finds few boards. Only 3 of the roughly 40 companies were genuine matches (Sarvam, Ema, Clueso). Four Ashby slugs belonged to unrelated foreign companies. An app should get the slug from the company's own careers page (the link to jobs.ashbyhq.com/X, *.keka.com and so on), not from the company name. It should also check that the board's company name and locations match.
- Indian early-stage startups seem to rely mostly on Keka, Zoho Recruit or their own careers pages. Neither Keka nor Zoho Recruit offers a documented public JSON API like Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse or Workable. Extending discovery to these would mean parsing HTML, and Keka pages are rendered by JavaScript.
- Technical internships seem most likely at small seed-stage AI startups in Bengaluru (RevRag.AI is the clear example). Larger, well-funded companies (Ema, Sarvam) mostly list experienced roles, plus some SDE-level ones.

- The roundups rarely give cities. Inc42 tables have no city column, and Entrackr gives only city totals. Cities above come from careers pages or ATS data where possible. Otherwise they are "n/s".

- Workable rate-limited most probes (HTTP 429, Cloudflare error 1015), so Workable results are inconclusive for most rows.
- I did not check the Keka and Zoho Recruit pages for job titles or intern roles, because they are JavaScript-rendered or too large to parse simply.
- Except for RapidCanvas and Ema, I did not read individual funding announcements to see how each company will use the money, including whether it goes to hiring.
- Clueso's $10 Mn Series A appears only in the Inc42 roundup. A web search found no separate article confirming it.

## Is there evidence that startups hire more engineers in the months after a funding round?

### Takeaway
There is some evidence: Revelio Labs reports that hiring rises before and after funding announcements. But the effect has weakened. Post-Series A hiring rates in 2024–25 were "several percentage points lower" than in 2020–22, and funding per employee has doubled. I found no India-specific study that measures hiring month by month after a round. For India, the evidence is company statements like RapidCanvas's plan to grow its India AI engineering team.

### Cited Findings
- Revelio Labs (US tech startups, Series A, 2020–2025): hiring rises in the months before and after a funding announcement, but it "remains muted relative to years past". Post-funding hiring rates in 2024–25 were "several percentage points lower" than in 2020–22. Median Series A headcount was 57 in 2020, 44 in 2024 and 47 in 2025 (partial year). Series A funding per employee rose from about $160k (2020) to more than $320k (2025). The article was published Oct 14, 2025, and does not describe its methodology in detail. [Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/news/tech/startups-are-hiring-less-and-raising-more)
- Tomasz Tunguz (2023): companies that raised top-quartile Seed, A, B or C rounds grew headcount by about 6% over the trailing twelve months. All other companies grew by about 12%. (This comes from a search summary; I did not fetch the page.) [tomtunguz.com](https://www.tomtunguz.com/headcount_growth_and_capital_raised/index.md)
- Carta (H2 2023): net headcount fell in 9 of the 13 months after November 2022, and new hires fell 49% in 2023. (Search summary.) [Carta](https://carta.com/data/startup-compensation-h2-2023/)
- India example: RapidCanvas (Series B, Oct 2026) says it will grow its "field AI engineering team across India, the US, and Brazil". [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/enterprise-ai-startup-rapidcanvas-nets-29-mn-to-expand-gtm-teams/). One outlet reported that it gave no hiring timeline. (Search summary.) [newskart](https://www.newskart.com/rapidcanvas-raises-29m-to-expand-ai-engineering-teams-in-india-lets-explore-it/)
- India example: Ema's $77 Mn Series B is going to go-to-market expansion. Ema says several senior leaders have already been hired. No Bengaluru headcount target was stated. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/ema-raises-77m-as-ai-starts-eating-into-enterprise-software-and-services/)
- Secondary or blog claim, not verified: startup job postings in India rose 12% year on year, from 116,080 (April 2025) to 130,010 (April 2026). The original data source is not named. [Fueler](https://fueler.io/blog/indian-startups-that-raised-funding-and-what-it-means-for-hiring)

### Inferences
- A new round is a reasonable but weak signal of hiring. For a student, a better trigger is a funding event combined with new postings on that company's board in the following weeks. An app can check this cheaply where a JSON board exists, using `publishedAt` on Ashby.
- Much of the new money in 2026 rounds is earmarked for go-to-market rather than engineering (RapidCanvas, Ema). An app could parse the announcement for words like "hiring", "engineering team" or "tech team".

### Gaps
- I found no India-specific study of hiring after funding, and no data on how many months the hiring lag lasts.
- I did not open the Tunguz and Carta pages myself, so their figures come only from search summaries.

## Which public, free, machine-readable sources of Indian funding news exist, and how reliable are they?

### Takeaway
Six RSS feeds loaded with fresh items on 2026-10-23: Inc42 (two feeds), Entrackr (`/rss`), YourStory, ET Tech and TechCrunch's India tag. The structured weekly lists are Inc42's "Funding Galore" weekly table (company, amount, stage, sector, lead investors, but no city) and YourStory's "Weekly funding roundup". Entrackr's weekly report is mostly prose and names only some companies. Funding headlines in these feeds follow a regular pattern ("X raises $N Mn in <stage> led by Y"), which an LLM or regex can extract. VCCircle's `/feed` returned 404. Moneycontrol's business RSS returned stale items.

### Inferences
- A practical design for the app: poll Inc42 `/buzz/feed/`, Entrackr `/rss`, YourStory `/feed` and ET Tech once a day. Filter for titles matching "raises|raised|funding|Series|Seed" and pull out company, amount, stage and lead investor with the LLM. Once a week, parse the Inc42 roundup table to fill in missed deals. This fits the project's "no polling workers" rule if it runs as a cron job or on demand.
- Inc42's robots signals ("ai-train=no", AI crawlers blocked) suggest it is fine to read headlines and link back, but storing or republishing full article text is a risk. Keep only extracted facts and the source URL.
- The feeds give the company name but no careers or ATS link. A second step (search for the company's careers page, then find its ATS slug) is needed, and the slug-collision problem shown above applies to it.

### Gaps
- I did not read the terms of use for Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory or ET. Only robots.txt was checked.
- I did not fetch the YourStory weekly roundup body to check whether it has a structured table.
- I did not test Tracxn, Crunchbase or VCCircle structured pages. They are usually paywalled or login-gated, but this was not verified.
- I did not find a funding-only ET RSS feed ID. The 78570540 feed returned general startup stories.
