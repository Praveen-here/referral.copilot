# LinkedIn and X "hiring posts" as a channel for India tech internships and fresher roles

Scope: India, tech roles, 2024 to Oct 2026 preferred. Research date 2026-10-24. Method: web search and third-party pages only. linkedin.com and x.com were never fetched. Labels: **[verified]** means I read the claim on the cited page myself. **[reported]** means it comes from a search snippet, a secondary write-up, or the original source's own unaudited claim. One limitation applies throughout: the search tool ignored `site:` operators, so I could not sample linkedin.com/posts or x.com/status URLs directly.

## Q1. Do Indian founders/CTOs post "we're hiring interns, DM me / comment / fill this form" on LinkedIn and X? Concrete examples

### Takeaway
Yes. Several well-covered Indian cases from 2024 to 2025 show founders and CEOs posting roles on X or LinkedIn with an informal way to apply: comment on the post, email a short intro, or tag someone. The best-covered ones are on X and went viral, which is why news outlets wrote about them. Ordinary low-engagement posts of this kind exist in large numbers, but news coverage and search results show very few of them.

### Cited Findings
- **Puch AI (Siddharth Bhatia, co-founder and CEO), X post dated Wed 6 Aug 2025.** Roles: AI Engineering Intern (full-time) and a "Growth Magician" intern. Remote, ₹1 to 2 lakh a month stipend, PPOs (pre-placement offers) for top performers, no degree needed, open to high schoolers. To apply, candidates **comment on the post** saying why they should be picked; DMs are discouraged. Tagging a candidate who gets hired wins an iPhone. Quote: "We hired a high schooler last month." **[verified]** [Storyboard18, 7 Aug 2025](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/indian-ai-startup-offers-remote-internship-with-rs-2-lakh-stipend-no-degree-required-78445.htm)
  - Business Today says applicants could comment on his LinkedIn **or** X post, and that the post drew about 1,000 likes and 500+ comments. **[reported, snippet]** [Business Today, 8 Aug 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/education/story/this-indian-ai-startup-is-offering-internships-with-rs-2-lakh-stipend-and-no-degree-needed-488583-2025-08-08)
- **Smallest AI (Sudarshan Kamath, founder, Bengaluru/Indiranagar), X post, covered 28 Feb 2025.** Role: full-stack engineer with 0 to 2 years of experience at ₹40 LPA, office 5 days a week. To apply, **email info@smallest.ai** with a 100-word intro and links to your best work. No resume needed, and college "does not matter". About 3.5 lakh views. No hiring outcome was reported. **[verified]** [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/business/bengaluru-startup-offers-rs-40-lpa-for-cracked-engineersno-resume-no-degree-required-44565)
- **Zomato (Deepinder Goyal), X post for a chief-of-staff role, Nov 2024.** 9.1 million views by 21 Nov, and Goyal said he had received 10,000 applications. Not a tech or intern role, but it shows the scale a founder's post can reach. **[reported, snippet]** [YourStory, Nov 2024](https://cwv.yourstory.com/2024/11/zomato-chief-of-staff-hiring-meme-fest-for-social-media)
- **Ordinary LinkedIn hiring posts with a form link.** The search index shows LinkedIn **profile** pages that carry reposted posts such as "🚀 We're Hiring Interns in Bangalore & Chennai!" with a form link and #Hiring #Internship tags, and a CodeCaste post asking people to email their resume to hireme@codecaste.com. **[reported, snippets only; linkedin.com not opened]** [in.linkedin.com/in/agil-p](https://in.linkedin.com/in/agil-p)
- Reddit communities such as r/IndiaInternshipDaily repost intern-hiring posts that say "comment or DM", and the moderators warn members to verify each one. **[reported, snippet]** [mirror of r/IndiaInternshipDaily](https://r.datuan.dev/r/IndiaInternshipDaily)
- Some Bengaluru startups list intern roles on their own careers pages instead, for example an AI equity-research startup offering ₹20 to 40k a month for 3 months from 1 Sep 2026, working directly with the founder, with a full-time offer for top performers. **[reported, snippet]** [binary.so](https://binary.so/uAjKWmQ)

### Inferences
- Founder posts come in three formats: (a) **comment to apply** (Puch AI), (b) **email a short intro and work links** (Smallest AI), (c) **form or email in the post** (ordinary LinkedIn posts). On viral posts, DMs are often discouraged because of the volume.
- The viral posts are rare outliers. A typical early-stage hiring post probably gets tens of comments and no news coverage, so news searches cannot show how common such posts are.
- Who posts: the examples I found were founders and CEOs (Puch, Smallest, Zomato) and HR or ops staff reposting roles (the LinkedIn snippets). I found no specific India examples of an engineering manager or CTO posting, though such posts are common anecdotally.

### Gaps
- No systematic sample of LinkedIn or X hiring posts by role (founder, CTO, EM, recruiter, employee), because the search tool ignored site: queries and linkedin.com and x.com were off-limits.
- No source on whether Puch AI or Smallest AI actually hired anyone through these posts. Bhatia's "we hired a high schooler last month" is his own claim.

## Q2. Data on what share of hires and internships come from LinkedIn posts vs LinkedIn Jobs vs referrals vs job boards

### Takeaway
I found no India survey that counts "LinkedIn posts" as a separate hiring channel, or that measures how many startup roles exist only as posts. The best recent figures are LinkedIn's Sept 2026 India Gen Z data: an inside connection makes a hire about 8.5x more likely, and about 30% of Gen Z found their next role through online professional communities.

### Cited Findings
- LinkedIn and Censuswide survey, July to August 2026: 18,050 professionals aged 18 to 80 in 12 markets including India. Released 2 Sep 2026. **[verified via ANI coverage]** [NewKerala/ANI](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/83-gen-professionals-india-hesitant-network-missing-hiring-779.htm)
  - Indian applicants with an inside connection are **~8.5x more likely to be hired** (LinkedIn data).
  - **~30% of Indian Gen Z found their next role through online professional communities.**
  - 83% are hesitant to network, 52% don't know how to start, 44% fear being judged, and 39% worry about bothering people.
  - Members who engaged with a connection's post were **~3x more likely to get a connection request and ~5x more likely to get a message** from that person.
  - 92% of Millennials and 95% of Gen X would help an unknown early-career person.
- JobBuzz/TimesJobs survey of 1,050 Indian graduate and postgraduate students: online job sites were rated most effective (45%), then social media sites (30%). LinkedIn is not broken out, and the survey is old (undated in the snippet, probably mid-2010s). **[reported, snippet]** [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/training-development/online-sites-most-effective-for-searching-internship-opportunities-15628)
- foundit: startup job postings up 32% year on year (Apr 2025), and 53% of startup jobs are aimed at freshers with 0 to 3 years of experience. These are posting counts, not channel shares. **[reported, snippet]** [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/indias-startup-hiring-surges-32-per-cent-sustainable-growth-new-focus-report-882862); [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/amp-startups-indias-startup-ecosystem-booms-freshers-in-high-demand-41229)
- TeamLease: Bengaluru leads fresher hiring intent at 81% for H2 2025, and e-commerce and tech startups lead at 88%. **[reported, snippet]** [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/e-commerce-tech-startups-lead-fresher-hiring-with-88-intent-in-h2-2025-teamlease-report-79134.htm)
- Internshala Internship Trends Report 2024: internship listings up 25% year on year. It covers supply only, not how students discover roles. **[reported, snippet]** [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/internshala-internship-trends-report-2024-25-percent-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024/amp)
- Practitioner guides say early Indian startups hire through the founder's network, referrals, and LinkedIn, GitHub and community outreach rather than Naukri-style boards, and widen channels only as they grow. These are opinions, not measurements. **[reported]** [eChai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/first-hires/where-do-i-actually-source-candidates-for-my-first-hires-without-a-recruiter-budget); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/resources/hire-right-technical-talent)
- Cutshort's comparison guides (written by a vendor) describe Wellfound as a board for early-stage inbound applications and LinkedIn Recruiter as the default tool for outreach to passive candidates. They contain no channel-share data. **[reported]** [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/best-platforms-to-hire-software-engineers-in-india)

### Inferences
- The 8.5x and "3x request / 5x message after engaging with a post" figures support an app strategy of warming up a contact (react to or comment on their post) before connecting and messaging, and of prioritising referrals over cold applications.
- "Openings that appear only as posts" is plausible for small startups with no ATS or Greenhouse/Lever board, but I found **no number** for it. Treat any figure as unknown.

### Gaps
- No LinkedIn Economic Graph India figure for hires via feed posts vs Jobs. No Naukri JobSpeak, Unstop, Wellfound or Instahyre channel-share data was found.
- The JobBuzz survey date could not be confirmed.

## Q3. Documented stories of Indian students or freshers hired by replying to a LinkedIn or X post or DMing the poster

### Takeaway
I found a few documented cases. Most are older (2021) or involve the candidate posting a pitch rather than replying to a hiring post. I found no well-documented India story from 2024 to 2026 of a student replying to a founder's tech-intern post and getting hired, other than the posters' own claims.

### Cited Findings
- **Pradologue (Pradarshan P), Substack, 8 Dec 2021.** He replied to a tweet on 17 Nov. Within about 20 minutes, Cutshort founder Nikunj Verma DMed him with a "Social Media Storyteller" role. After two calls he was hired as a "Storytelling Consultant" under a signed agreement. The company is Indian. Not a tech role. **[verified]** [How I got a job on Twitter](https://pradologue.substack.com/p/how-i-got-a-job-on-twitter-)
- **CRED design intern, June 2021.** A 21-year-old Mumbai designer posted a 3D video application on LinkedIn tagging Kunal Shah. Shah replied, and CRED's design team took him on as an intern. This is the candidate posting a pitch, not replying to a hiring post. **[reported, snippet]** [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/amp/latest/trends/story/21-year-old-makes-innovative-pitch-for-internship-on-linkedin-gets-hired-297805-2021-06-08)
- **Xiaomi India digital-marketing intern (Vidya Academy EEE student, 2020).** He applied through LinkedIn. Over one lakh students applied, 25 were shortlisted, and he was selected. This was likely a LinkedIn Jobs application, not a post. **[reported, snippet]** [Vidya Academy news](https://news.vidyaacademy.ac.in/2020/10/29/eee-student-gets-internship-at-xioami-india/)
- Puch AI CEO: "We hired a high schooler last month" (Aug 2025). This is the poster's own claim about his hiring channel, and the article does not confirm the hire came from a post. **[verified that he said it]** [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/indian-ai-startup-offers-remote-internship-with-rs-2-lakh-stipend-no-degree-required-78445.htm)

### Inferences
- In these cases the response came fast (within 20 minutes) and the poster started the DM. Replying publicly and specifically to a founder's post can lead the founder to message you first.

### Gaps
- No 2024 to 2026 India tech-intern success story found via post reply. YouTube descriptions, Medium and X threads were not reachable through this search tool in a usable way.

## Q4. Indian tech X ("build in public", founders posting roles) vs LinkedIn: how active?

### Takeaway
X carries the high-signal, often viral founder hiring posts from Bengaluru AI and dev-tool startups (Puch AI, Smallest AI, Zomato's CoS post). LinkedIn carries far more volume, including routine HR and recruiter posts, form links, and most of the scam posts. I found no data that compares the two platforms' volumes.

### Cited Findings
- All three of the most-covered 2024 to 2025 India founder hiring posts I found were on X (Puch AI was also cross-posted to LinkedIn). **[verified for Puch/Smallest; reported for Zomato]** Sources as in Q1.
- In July 2025 the Soham Parekh episode (a candidate allegedly working at several startups at once) began with a founder's X post that got 17k+ likes in a day. It led many founders to share their hiring stories on X, which shows that X is where startup founders talk about hiring. **[reported, snippet]** [Know Your Meme](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/events/soham-parekh-yc-controversy)

### Inferences
- For an app targeting **startup** roles, founder X posts are a high-value but low-volume source. LinkedIn posts are higher-volume and noisier.

### Gaps
- No quantitative comparison of hiring posts on LinkedIn vs X in India.

## Q5. Are these posts discoverable via Google (and so via Serper)? How fast are they indexed?

### Takeaway
Only partly. Google indexes **some** linkedin.com/posts and x.com/status pages, but coverage is low and uneven, favouring authors with high engagement or large followings. No reliable source gives indexing latency. A Serper-only app will see a small, delayed, popularity-biased slice of hiring posts.

### Cited Findings
- LinkedIn posts: one practitioner found only about **10% of his short-form LinkedIn posts were indexed** by Google, and it is unclear how Google chooses which ones. Google has made no official statement about indexing LinkedIn posts. **[reported, anecdotal]** [Boolean Strings](https://booleanstrings.com/?p=4314); [Lireo](https://www.lireo.com/think-twice-before-only-publishing-your-posts-on-linkedin/)
- SEO practitioner Jesper Nissen says normal status posts can rank "extremely well if you can get them to index", but Pulse articles no longer index for him. Google often uses the first 7 to 12 words of a post as its title, and hashtags at the start may become the title. **[reported, practitioner opinion]** [FatRank podcast transcript](https://fatrank.transistor.fm/435/transcript)
- LinkedIn www pages dropped out of Google for about 10 hours in 2020 and came back quickly because they had been hidden, not deindexed. This says nothing about normal indexing latency. **[reported]** [SE Roundtable](https://seroundtable.com/linkedin-google-return-29413.html)
- X/Twitter: historically Google indexed only **0.6% to 3.4%** of tweets, skewed toward high-authority accounts (2015 study). By Oct 2016 indexed volume had fallen to about 64% of its May 2015 peak. **[reported]** [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-indexing-only-3-4-of-tweets-mostly-from-influencers-study/136497); [Perficient](https://blogs.perficient.com/2017/01/30/why-googles-indexing-of-tweets-is-shrinking-heres-why-103); a separate study found about 96% of tweets not indexed **[reported]** [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/google-referencement-twitter/)
- 2023: a Google spokesperson said crawling of twitter.com was restricted, making tweets harder to show in results. X later blocked Bingbot in robots.txt. **[reported]** [Gigazine citing The Verge](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20230704-twitter-google-search); [OnMSFT](https://onmsft.com/?p=324706)
- Practical sign: in this research, viral X hiring posts were found through **news coverage** (Storyboard18, People Matters, Business Today, YourStory), not through x.com results. LinkedIn content showed up mainly as **in.linkedin.com/in/<profile>** pages that carry snippets of recent posts or reposts, not as /posts/ URLs. **[verified for this tool; Serper/Google may differ]**

### Inferences
- For the app: Serper queries like `"hiring" "intern" <company>` with LinkedIn post or profile URLs will return mostly older posts with higher engagement. Fresh posts from the last 24 to 72 hours will likely be missing. News coverage acts as a second, slower index for viral X posts.
- The app could use Serper's date filter (tbs=qdr:w or m) and treat any post-derived opening as "unverified lead, check manually by opening the URL in your browser". That stays within the rule never to fetch LinkedIn.

### Gaps
- No 2024 to 2026 measurement of Google's indexing rate or delay for linkedin.com/posts or x.com/status. If the number matters, the app team must measure it: query Serper for a sample of known recent posts on a daily schedule.

## Q6. Fake or engagement-bait "hiring" posts and how to tell real ones

### Takeaway
Common and well documented in India. The "comment 'interested' → 'inbox me' → paid resume review, referral or course" funnel is the standard scam. Real founder posts name the company, the role and a company email or form, and ask for nothing that leads to payment.

### Cited Findings
- **Decode (BOOM), 13 Sep 2024, updated 6 Oct 2025.** Scammers post job or referral ads that name Deloitte, McKinsey, IBM or Barclays and ask people to **comment "interested"**. Commenters get an "Inbox me" and are moved into DMs, then offered paid services (e.g. a ~₹1,000 resume review via UPI), fake referrals, or courses (one victim was offered a ₹45,000 course). Some push victims to WhatsApp or Telegram. The poster accounts often list no real job history at the companies they name. Pages selling fake internship certificates (Encryptix ~21k followers, CodeAlpha ~195k) failed verification checks. Deloitte says it never charges candidates fees. **[verified]** [Decode](https://www.decodeinternet.in/decode/linkedin-job-scams-fake-resume-reviews-upskilling-trap-job-seekers-fake-job-offers-recruitment-fraud-26455)
- Comment-bait is used to game the feed algorithm, and email addresses posted in comments get scraped and resold. Scammers use big company names to build trust. **[reported]** [Ulektz, Mar 2025](https://news.ulektz.com/2025/03/04/7-shocking-reasons-why-job-post-scam/amp/)
- Nestlé India's chairman publicly disowned a fake LinkedIn "bulk hiring" post that offered ₹48,000 a month and carried a suspicious link. **[reported, snippet]** [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/nestles-chairman-flags-fake-linkedin-job-post-warns-against-scams-142208.html)
- In India, scammers use tech jobs as bait because of the sector's size and pay. **[reported]** [Schneier on Security, Dec 2025](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2025/12/linkedin-job-scams.html)
- A fresher's account describes "genuine-looking" LinkedIn listings that led to a paid internship webinar. **[reported, snippet]** [Hashnode tag page](https://hashnode.com/tag/it-jobs-in-india)
- Resume Builder survey: 4 in 10 recruiters said they had posted a fake job (cited by Decode). **[reported]** [Decode](https://www.decodeinternet.in/decode/linkedin-job-scams-fake-resume-reviews-upskilling-trap-job-seekers-fake-job-offers-recruitment-fraud-26455)

### Inferences (red-flag checklist for the app)
- **Likely fake or bait:** "comment interested" combined with no named company or role; the poster is not verifiably employed at the company (no matching Experience entry, which the app's verify.ts already checks); a push to WhatsApp or Telegram; any fee, UPI request or "certificate"; mass referral offers for many big brands; "inbox me" replies.
- **Likely real:** the post comes from a founder, CTO or employee whose profile shows the company; names a specific role, stipend and location; gives a company-domain email, a Greenhouse/Lever/careers-page link, or a clear comment instruction from the founder (Puch AI style); and gets independent coverage or a careers-page match.

### Gaps
- No figure for the share of India "hiring" posts that are bait. No LinkedIn data on this specific pattern.
