# Indian product companies that hire technical interns and freshers in India (as of 23 Oct 2026)

Method note: every ATS endpoint below was fetched live on 2026-10-23 with a script (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters public APIs, plus Workday's public `wday/cxs` JSON search for BrowserStack and Sprinklr). Careers-page HTML was fingerprinted for ATS links (Workday, Darwinbox, TurboHire, etc.). "Verified" = I saw it in a live ATS response or a company-owned page today. "Reported" = third-party source (aggregator, X post, interview-experience site); dated where possible. Lever `createdAt` timestamps were converted from epoch ms. Web search results were mostly aggregators (myinternships.in, coursejoiner, freshershunt, etc.); I flag them as low reliability.

Important caveat on myinternships.in: its listings for Razorpay, PhonePe, Zerodha and CRED reuse near-identical template text ("6-month, fully mentored internship", "Minimum 60% / 6.0 CGPA (relaxable...)", "4 stages", "22K/month"). That pattern suggests machine-generated or templated listings, so I treat them as weak evidence. They are not proof that a live posting exists.

## Which companies hired technical interns or freshers in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Live public ATS boards on 23 Oct 2026 show very few **technical** intern postings at Indian product companies. Verified live tech-intern postings: **Hevo Data** (Associate SDE Intern, 21 Sep 2026) and **FamPay** (Security Engineer Intern, 23 Sep 2026). Most big unicorns (Flipkart, PhonePe, Zerodha, CRED, Swiggy tech, Zepto, Juspay, Zoho) run hiring on their own career sites, on Indian ATSs without public JSON (TurboHire, Darwinbox), or through campus programs and hiring challenges (Flipkart GRiD, Juspay Hiring Challenge, Myntra Ramp Up, Atlan intern cohorts). Their intern hiring is real but seasonal. Reported 2025-2026 tech-intern hiring with reasonable evidence: Flipkart, Myntra, Swiggy, ShareChat, Atlan, Juspay, CRED, Zoho, Cars24, Razorpay, PhonePe, Postman (campus).

## Off-campus vs campus, public postings, which years, stipends

Off-campus routes exist mainly as national contests or challenges (Flipkart GRiD, Juspay Hiring Challenge, Myntra Ramp Up via Unstop) and as smaller-company public postings (Hevo, FamPay, ShareChat on Instahyre, Atlan cohorts). 2nd/3rd-year students are explicitly eligible at Flipkart GRiD (2027-2030 batches in the 2026 cycle), Cars24 AI Intern (3rd-year 2027 grads) and CRED's 2027-batch internship.
