# Public job feeds miss most Indian tech internships

On 2026-10-23, live public applicant tracking system (ATS) feeds showed real technical intern or new-grad roles in India at only a few dozen companies. Big tech and MNCs: Google, Amazon (SDE I), Stripe, Rubrik, HackerRank, HPE, Adobe, NVIDIA and Deutsche Bank. Indian product companies and startups: Hevo Data, FamPay, CloudSEK, Neuron7 and AiPrise. RevRag.AI, a seed-funded startup, listed five 6-month technical internships with a PPO on its own careers page. The companies with the strongest record of recurring intern hiring are mostly not pollable at all. Flipkart (GRiD contest), Juspay (Hiring Challenge), ShareChat, Zoho, Swiggy, Atlan, PhonePe, Postman and Zerodha hire through contests, campus drives, their own JavaScript careers sites, Indian ATSs with no public feed (TurboHire, Darwinbox), or third-party portals. Among about 30 startup boards with live India jobs, only 4 had a technical intern open. Well-funded AI startups (Sarvam, Ema, Composio, TrueFoundry) post many India engineering roles, but their India interns are non-technical. A monitor built only on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday will catch roughly 40 to 50% of a well-known Indian company list. Its main value is different: it shows which companies have a live India engineering team and take juniors, which is exactly who a referral app should target. The rest of this report covers who hires, where the feeds are, how to reach people where nothing is posted, and how to build the early-posting monitor and funded-startup tracker without touching LinkedIn.

A note on evidence. "Verified" means a researcher fetched the endpoint or page on 2026-10-23 and saw the data. "Reported" means a third-party source (aggregator, X post, interview-experience site, vendor survey) that could not be confirmed at the source. A board with zero intern titles today means only that none were open that day. Most programs are seasonal.

About 15 stayed unresolved because their pages render with JavaScript. A separate count among targeted funded and YC startups found about 40% with a fetchable board. Freshteam is being shut down: renewals stop from 7 Mar 2026, and access ends by April 2027 at the latest ([People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/amp/news/business/freshworks-to-end-freshteam-hr-product-stop-renewals-from-march-2026-47939)). Indian job boards should be paste-only sources. Naukri's terms forbid automated crawling ([Naukri T&C](https://www.naukri.com/termsconditions)), and Internshala and Unstop disallow `/api/` paths in robots.txt.

Slug guessing is the biggest data-quality trap. These slugs returned live boards that belong to unrelated foreign companies:

- **Greenhouse:** `slice` (Skopje/UK firm, not the Indian fintech), `clear`, `zetaglobal`, `nurix`, `pronto`, `porter`, `kite`.
- **Lever:** `fi` (US), `trueml` (Latin America).
- **Ashby:** `navi` (SF hardware), `bolt`, `lightspeed`, `nudge` (SF neurotech), `confido` (NYC CPG), `circle`, `sol` (San Mateo).

Workable answered with company names "origin", "Crest", "DSW" and "Centricity" but empty lists, which proves nothing ([GH slice](https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/slice/jobs), [Ashby navi](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/navi), [Ashby nudge](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/nudge)). For this reason, the companion CSV leaves out the rows for slice, Origin, Confido Health, Circle and Sol. Some boards are stale even though they answer: Eternal's Lever board last posted on 2025-07-30, and SmartRecruiters boards for Unacademy and Cars24 date from 2020 and 2018. The InMobi board also showed that Greenhouse `metadata` can leak an employee email, so the app should never store raw metadata.

## Where nothing is posted, the route is a person: founders, alumni and contests

Startups do hire students, including for long and full-time-style internships. Fresher hiring intent was highest at e-commerce and tech startups: **88%** in H2 2025, against 70% overall (TeamLease, n = 1,065 employers), with Bengaluru at 81% ([PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/recruiting-and-onboarding/e-commerce-and-tech-start-ups-push-fresher-hiring-intent-to-88percent-in-h2-2025-43039), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/e-commerce-tech-startups-lead-fresher-hiring-with-88-intent-in-h2-2025-teamlease-report-79134.htm)). Startup listings typically run 3 to 6 months and target current students. Lane, for example, takes the 2026 to 2028 batches and pays ₹15k, and Scaler's AI engineer intern pays ₹50k to 60k with a PPO ([The First Pass #27](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-27-pick-a-startup)). Internshala's 2024 platform data shows 49% of internships were work-from-home and about 22 to 23% carried a PPO, with an average stipend of Rs 8,000 ([BW People](https://www.bwpeople.in/article/25-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024-internshala-report-542289), [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/internshala-internship-trends-report-2024-25-percent-rise-in-internship-opportunities-observed-in-2024)). "Startups don't care about degrees" goes too far. Listings ask for proof of work (GitHub, demos) on top of batch and degree filters ([jobfound listing](https://jobfound.org/job/waterscience-is-hiring-for-founders-office-intern-ai-tech-bengaluru-karnataka-india-india-21-october-2026)).

Founder outreach is the recommended route at small Indian startups, though no India reply-rate data exists. An Indian guide says "Founder directly decide karta hai" ("the founder decides directly") and advises contacting the founder or CTO at startups and an engineering manager at mid-size firms ([Jobrise](https://jobrise.io/en/blog/internship-kaise-milegi-tips/)). Several listings ask applicants to email a GitHub profile or project instead of only a resume ([The First Pass #28](https://thefirstpass.substack.com/p/the-first-pass-28-the-vibe-shift)). The only reply-rate figure is a non-Indian anecdote: 17 positive replies from 150 cold emails, or 11.3% ([firstsales.io](https://firstsales.io/blog/cold-emails-for-internships/)). The message shape these sources support has four parts: why this company, one relevant project link, a specific ask (for example "6 months, full time, from January"), and a resume.

On channels, the evidence argues against open WhatsApp groups and for verified relationships. A May 2025 LocalCircles survey found **42%** of Indian WhatsApp users had received fake job offers demanding payment ([mediaindia.eu](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)). IIT Bhubaneswar warned about a fake internship scheme spreading through WhatsApp groups ([IIT BBS](https://www.iitbbs.ac.in/?p=31569)). Scammers also run fake LinkedIn profiles and startup pages ([Dinamalar](https://www.dinamalar.com/news/kalvimalar-news-en/fraudsters-target-college-students-with-fake-internship-offers-warn-cyber-experts/57787)). The notes did not find any data comparing reply rates across WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email or X. The inference is that your own college's alumni groups beat open "job alert" groups. For a stranger, a work email to a founder or a LinkedIn note (sent manually) is the normal channel.

No survey ranks channels by the offers they produce, so the picture has to be pieced together:

- **Contests:** Flipkart GRiD and the Juspay challenge are proven intern pipelines. More broadly, 70% of Gen Z join hackathons and case contests, but only 25% of recruiters treat them as a primary hiring tool ([Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/jobs/story/83-engineering-46-b-school-grads-remain-without-a-job-internship-offer-unstop-report-468828-2025-03-21)).
- **Off-campus GCC hiring:** about 40% of GCC entry-level hiring is reportedly off-campus (second-hand) ([placementpreparation.io](https://www.placementpreparation.io/blog/off-campus-recruitment-process-india/)).
- **Referrals:** they are heavy in India, with about 65% of organisations using them, but that figure is a 2015 survey ([PeopleMatters](https://www.peoplematters.in/amp-recruitment-least-65-recruiters-india-use-employee-referral-programmes-12311)). No data exists on referral-to-interview rates for interns.
- **GSoC:** best treated as proof of work rather than a pipeline. There were 1,220 contributors from 43,984 applicants in 2024 ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2024/05/11/dcm9-newton-school-of-technology.html)).

For campus-centric firms such as Postman, PhonePe and Freshworks, a referral is likely the only off-campus path. Eternal (Zomato) reportedly takes applications through employee referrals, though this is unconfirmed ([fueler](https://fueler.io/blog/zomato-hiring-process-how-to-get-a-job-at-zomato)).

## October is late for big-tech summer roles and early for 6-month startup intakes

A 2026 hiring calendar describes typical patterns rather than confirmed dates ([Let's Code](https://www.lets-code.co.in/blogs/india-off-campus-hiring-calendar-2026/)):

- **Aug to Oct:** summer internships for the following year at big tech and banks (FAANG-style applications run Aug to Dec).
- **Jul and Sep:** two peaks for product and startup fresher hiring.
- **Oct:** quant and finance firms.
- **Nov:** a last push (Atlassian, Uber, Walmart, Salesforce winter intake).
- **Dec:** the slowest month.
- **Jan to May:** service-company mass drives.

Banks go earliest. Goldman Sachs opened India 2027 Summer Analyst applications on 1 Jul 2026 ([QuintEdge](https://quintedge.com/blog/investment-banking-internships-india)). One guide puts the peak for structured programs in Jan to Feb, so the sources partly conflict ([Saarthi](https://joinsaarthi.com/blogs/summer-internship-2026-india-complete-application-guide)). The verified data fits the calendar: PhD and research interns were live in October, and Postman's India intern deadline is projected for about late Jan 2027 ([Extern](https://www.extern.com/post/postman-internship-guide)). For a student on 23 Oct 2026, this means two things. Remaining big-tech summer windows are closing. Startup 6-month January-start internships are the live opportunity for November and December outreach.

Ghost and scam postings are common enough to filter for:

- **Fake postings:** Kroll estimates about 20% of Indian job postings are suspected fake ([mediaindia.eu](https://mediaindia.eu/society/silent-rejections-ghosting-disrupts-indias-job-market/)).
- **Stale postings:** a vendor scan of 29,815 Indian postings in Sep 2026 found about 1 in 4 was older than 90 days ([JobTether](https://jobtether.com/blog/ghost-jobs-india)).
- **Ghosting:** 73% of 1,000 online respondents said they had been ghosted (Apr 2024).
- **Losses:** digital frauds, including fake job offers, cost Indians ₹4,245 crore in the first 10 months of FY2024-25 ([dailyfinancial.in](https://dailyfinancial.in/caught-in-a-trap-fake-job-scams-india-drain-%E2%82%B94245-crore-stay-safe/)).

The scam pattern is consistent. Victims are "hired" with no interview, then asked for a "clearance" or "training" fee via UPI. In a Chennai case, students offered a ₹15,000 stipend were asked to pay ₹2,000 ([Dinamalar](https://www.dinamalar.com/news/kalvimalar-news-en/fraudsters-target-college-students-with-fake-internship-offers-warn-cyber-experts/57787)). AICTE's official internship portal is free for students ([CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/features/aicte-internship-scams-new-2026-rules-to-identify-fake-offers-and-protect-your-career-011-65999.html)). On the ATS side, real boards create their own ghost risk. FamPay still shows a "Copy Intern" posting created in 2020, so a posting's age should count as a signal.

## Building the monitor: poll by first-seen ID, and treat funding as a weak trigger

The early-posting monitor should start with Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby. They are documented, carry dates, can be cached, and between them cover most YC and AI startups plus many GCCs. Workday CXS comes next, since it holds most MNC India intern postings (HPE, Adobe, NVIDIA, Deutsche Bank, BrowserStack, Sprinklr). SmartRecruiters (Bosch, Swiggy, Freshworks, ServiceNow) and amazon.jobs follow. Keka and Zoho come last because they are fragile. The repo currently limits the job-posting reader to Greenhouse and Lever (CLAUDE.md), so adding Ashby, Workday or SmartRecruiters needs an explicit decision and a `docs/DECISIONS.md` entry. Google offers only HTML. Microsoft, Darwinbox, TurboHire and Eightfold stay paste-only, and so do Naukri, Internshala and Unstop.

Several design points follow from the endpoint behaviour:

- **New-posting date:** use the time an ID first appears in a feed. Vendor dates are only a cross-check, because Ashby's `publishedAt` resets on republish and Workday lists only relative dates.
- **India filter:** Lever `country`, SmartRecruiters `location.country`, Ashby `addressCountry`, Workday detail `country.descriptor`, and city-name matching for Greenhouse.
- **Intern and junior filter:** Ashby `employmentType: Intern` plus title words (intern, apprentice, trainee, SDE I, Engineer I, new grad, graduate, university).
- **Polling cost:** conditional GETs with `If-None-Match` for Greenhouse and Ashby make polling every 30 to 60 minutes nearly free. Workday needs one POST with `limit: 20` a few times a day, with detail calls only for new IDs.
- **Project rules:** the no-polling-workers rule and Neon's ~150 ms round trips point to one cron or on-demand batch. It should fetch all feeds concurrently (about 2 requests per host), diff IDs in memory, and write once.
- **Slug discovery:** take the slug from the careers page link or from the apply link of a job the user pastes, never from the company name. Accept a board only if its company name and India locations match. Detect empty or zero-count responses (Workable, SmartRecruiters) as "not found".

The most useful output is not "new intern posted". It is "this company has a live India engineering board with junior titles". That flag should feed straight into the app's people-finding and referral flow, because most intern seats never appear on any board.

The funded-startup tracker can run on verified free RSS feeds: Inc42 `/buzz/feed/`, Entrackr `/rss` (not `/feed`), YourStory `/feed` and ET Tech `rssfeeds/13357270.cms`, polled once a day. The feed list comes from [the Inc42 weekly roundup](https://inc42.com/buzz/from-boldfit-to-origin-indian-startups-raised-about-236-mn-this-week/) and [Entrackr's report index](https://entrackr.com/report/weekly-funding-report). Headlines follow the pattern "X raises $N Mn in <stage> led by Y", so an LLM or regex can extract company, amount, stage and investor. Inc42's weekly table can fill gaps but has no city column. Outlets disagree on figures: Simple Energy's Series C was $180 Mn per Inc42 and $182 Mn per Entrackr, so keep the source URL with each fact. Inc42's robots.txt sets `ai-train=no` and blocks AI crawlers, so the app should store extracted facts and links, not article text ([Inc42 robots](https://inc42.com/robots.txt)).

Funding is a weak hiring signal on its own. Revelio Labs finds hiring rises around US Series A announcements but is "muted relative to years past", and Series A funding per employee doubled to more than $320k by 2025 ([Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/news/tech/startups-are-hiring-less-and-raising-more)). The notes found no India study. In the 2026 rounds that were read, much of the money went to go-to-market, as at Ema and RapidCanvas ([Inc42 RapidCanvas](https://inc42.com/buzz/enterprise-ai-startup-rapidcanvas-nets-29-mn-to-expand-gtm-teams/)). Most roundup companies are D2C, EV or hardware. Only 3 of about 40 tech-leaning funded companies resolved to a genuine guessed board, and 4 of the guessed Ashby slugs belonged to other companies. The tracker should therefore score a round higher when the announcement mentions engineering hiring and when new India postings appear on the company's board in the following weeks. It should then send the user to people at the company rather than wait for an intern posting.

## Conclusion

Visibility and opportunity do not line up in Indian tech internship hiring. The best-documented feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) mostly hold senior engineers and non-technical interns. The companies most likely to take a student either post only during contests and campus seasons or never post at all. The opportunity that is actually visible in late October is narrow: a few PhD-heavy big-tech roles, a handful of small-company intern and SDE I postings, and six-month January intakes at startups. So the monitor should not be judged by how many internships it surfaces. It should be judged by how early it flags a company with a live India engineering team and junior titles, and how quickly the app then finds the alumnus or engineer there to ask.

Two risks matter more than coverage: false matches and false postings. Slug collisions, stale boards and scam offers each break trust. A monitor that verifies company identity, ages postings by first-seen time and never ingests open WhatsApp or job-board content will be more useful than a larger one that does. Three things remain unknown and are worth measuring inside the app: India reply rates by channel, referral-to-interview conversion for interns, and the lag between a funding round and new engineering postings. The app is well placed to measure all three from its own outreach data.
